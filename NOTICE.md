# Third-party data and attribution

`kotoba-anki` is MIT-licensed software. This repository does **not** distribute
the vocabulary, dictionary, lexical, or frequency datasets used by the tool.
Users obtain those datasets separately and remain responsible for complying
with their licences.

The project has been developed and tested with:

- **OpenJLPT** — JLPT N5–N1 vocabulary metadata. OpenJLPT is distributed under
  CC BY-SA 4.0 and includes its own upstream attribution.
  <https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT>
- **JMdict / EDRDG** — lexical readings, written forms, restrictions, priority
  and part-of-speech evidence. JMdict is made available by the Electronic
  Dictionary Research and Development Group under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  <https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/newlic.html>
- **Japanese Wiktionary / Kaikki / Wiktextract-derived data** — the tested
  Japanese StarDict dictionary was built from Japanese Wiktionary-derived data.
  Wiktionary-derived data is subject to Wiktionary's applicable licences,
  including CC BY-SA.
  <https://kaikki.org/jawiktionary/index.html>
- **Jiten** — optional frequency ordering. Jiten states that its downloadable
  frequency lists and other derived data are CC BY-SA 4.0.
  <https://jiten.moe/about>

## Generated decks

A generated `.apkg` or CSV can contain or adapt material from the user's input
datasets. If you redistribute generated decks or data, review and comply with
the licences and attribution requirements of every dataset you used.

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` applies only to the `kotoba-anki` source code and
repository-owned documentation; it does not relicense third-party data.
